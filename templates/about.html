About TribePlan

One place for your mental, physical, nutritional, and social health – built by someone who's had to work on all four.

TribePlan is a personal project by Conor, born from years of juggling training, mood, food, and friendships in different apps and never quite seeing the full picture.

Why TribePlan exists

I built TribePlan because I never saw my health as just one thing. There were seasons where training was great but my head wasn't right. Other times I was eating well but felt disconnected from people. Trying to juggle different apps and spreadsheets for mood, workouts, food, and friends felt messy and overwhelming.

TribePlan is my answer to that – a single place where you can see how your mind, body, nutrition, and social life fit together. It's deliberately simple, focused on habits and awareness rather than perfection or streaks for the sake of streaks.

This matters to me personally because I've struggled with all of these areas at different points. Building TribePlan is my way of turning that experience into something useful – a calm, encouraging space that helps you feel a little more in control of your day.

About Conor

I'm Conor, a student and lifelong learner who loves training, tech, and building things that actually help people.

Sport, coaching, and community have been a huge part of my life – but so have patches of anxiety, burnout, and feeling like I was never quite "on top" of my health.

TribePlan is the app I wish I'd had growing up – something that shows the full picture and makes taking care of yourself feel less lonely.

Mental

Mood & Headspace

Daily check-ins, journaling, and Aura AI reflections help you notice patterns – not to judge yourself, but to understand what lifts you up or drags you down.

Physical

Movement & Recovery

Manual logs, Strava sync, and wearable data (steps, sleep, heart rate) give you a real view of how you move, rest, and recover – beyond just chasing numbers.

Nutrition

Fuel, Not Rules

The food tracker is built to be realistic: simple logging, clear targets, and no guilt. The goal is to help you fuel training, mood, and life – not obsess over every gram.

Social

Your Tribe

The buddy system is there because health is easier with people. Matching by location, goals, and activity level makes it less awkward to find someone to train, walk, or check in with.

Where this is going

TribePlan will keep evolving – tighter wearable sync, smarter prompts from Aura AI, and more ways to show your progress without turning your life into a leaderboard. The core promise stays the same: gentle accountability, clear insights, and a focus on the whole person.

If you're using TribePlan, thank you. I'm building this for people like us – who want to feel better, perform better, and still live a real, messy, human life.
