TribePlan topic videos
=====================

Place your MP4 files here. The app looks for these names (first found is used):

  Mood / journal:   Mood.mp4, Mood2.mp4, or mood-bg.mp4
  Fitness:          Fitness.mp4, Fitness2.mp4, or fitness-bg.mp4
  Nutrition:        Nutrition.mp4, Nutrition2.mp4, or nutrition-bg.mp4

If none are found for a topic, demo-screen.mp4 is used.

Tips:
- Keep clips short (e.g. 10–30 seconds); they loop.
- Use muted, low-motion clips for best performance.
- Landscape or square works best (they are cropped to cover the area).
